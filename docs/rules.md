# Rules

## Game Mechanics
The game requires a minimum of 4 players. Roles are divided into sides such as Town, Mafia, and Third Party.

## Role Rules
- **Mafia Roles:** Must work together to eliminate the town. (e.g. Godfather, Matador, Saul Goodman)
- **Town Roles:** Must find the mafia during the day. (e.g. Doctor, Detective, Citizen, Constantine, Leon)
- **Third Party Roles:** Have their own win conditions. (e.g. Nostradamus)

Roles are constrained by limits and balanced according to the selected mode. The application automatically validates if the balance between Mafia and Town exceeds the mode's configured limits.
