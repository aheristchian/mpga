# How to Play

Mafia Party Game flow:
1. **Mode Selection**: Choose the ruleset for your game (e.g., Godfather, Classic Mafia).
2. **Setup**: Add at least 4 players and arrange their seating order.
3. **Role Selection**: Select roles to include in the game based on the number of players.
4. **Playing Phase**: The moderator guides the players through day and night phases, using the app to track living/dead players and active abilities.
